Frequently Asked Questions

What is CTRPRE?
CTRPRE is a personal investment companion app for iPhone. It helps you track global equities, log trades, manage watchlists, build portfolios, and use financial calculators — all in one elegant, privacy-first app.
Does CTRPRE require an account?
No. CTRPRE requires no account, sign-in, or registration. All data stays on your device.
Is my financial data private?
Yes. All your trades, notes, photos, and watchlists are stored locally on your device. We never upload your data to any server.
Is CTRPRE financial advice?
No. CTRPRE is an educational and informational tool only. It does not provide financial, investment, or tax advice. Always consult a qualified professional before making investment decisions.
What iOS version is required?
CTRPRE requires iOS 16 or later and runs on iPhone only.
How do I delete my data?
You can delete any trade entry or photo individually within the app. To remove everything, simply uninstall the app from your device.
How do I report a bug or request a feature?
Please email us at the support address above with a detailed description and, if possible, screenshots. We appreciate your feedback.
