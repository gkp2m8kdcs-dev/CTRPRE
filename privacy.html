Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 11, 2026

CTRPRE ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our iOS application CTRPRE (the "App").

1. Information We Collect

CTRPRE is designed with privacy as a core principle. The App does not require account creation, sign-in, or any form of user registration. We do not collect, store, or transmit personal information to our servers.

The App may collect the following types of information locally on your device only:

  • Trade entries (quantity, price, fees, notes, timestamps)
  • Watchlist symbols and portfolio allocations
  • Photo attachments you choose to attach to trades
  • App preferences and settings

2. Data Storage

All user-generated data is stored exclusively on your device using iOS local storage (UserDefaults and the app's sandboxed file system). We do not have remote servers that store your personal data. Your data never leaves your device unless you explicitly export it.

3. Permissions

The App may request the following iOS permissions, used solely for the stated purpose:

  • Camera — to capture photos of trade receipts or notes that you attach to your trade entries.
  • Photo Library — to attach existing photos to your trade entries.

Photos are stored locally on your device only. We do not upload them to any server.

4. Analytics and Tracking

CTRPRE does not use third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking technologies. We do not track your activity across other apps or websites.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because no data is collected from any user, this policy applies universally.

6. Data Security

Your data benefits from iOS's built-in security features, including device-level encryption when a passcode is enabled. Since all data stays on your device, the security of your information is tied to your device security.

7. Your Rights and Choices

Because all data is stored locally on your device, you have full control at all times. You can:

  • Delete any trade entry or photo within the App.
  • Remove the App to delete all associated data from your device.
  • Disable Camera or Photo Library permissions at any time via iOS Settings.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes indicates acceptance of the revised policy.
